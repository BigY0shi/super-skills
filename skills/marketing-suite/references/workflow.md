# Marketing Suite — 8-Phase Campaign Workflow

Use this workflow for complex multi-step campaigns or when the user asks for a full marketing plan.

---

## The Pipeline

**Research → Insights → Creative → Plan → Create → Edit → Publish → Measure**

---

## Phase 1: Research

**Goal**: Understand the market, audience, and competitive landscape before any creative work.

**Actions**:
- Market analysis: target segments, market size, trends
- Competitor analysis: messaging, channels, positioning gaps
- Customer research: ICP definition, pain points, language
- Keyword research: organic opportunities

**Tools**: customer-research skill, seo-mastery skill (keyword research section)
**Output**: Research reports with findings + strategic implications

---

## Phase 2: Insights

**Goal**: Synthesize research into actionable insights and opportunities.

**Actions**:
- Audience segmentation with priority order
- Competitive positioning opportunities (gaps to own)
- Channel opportunities with evidence
- Message framework: core value prop + supporting messages per segment

**Output**: Insights summary + recommended positioning

---

## Phase 3: Creative

**Goal**: Generate ideas and establish the creative direction.

**Actions**:
- Campaign concept / big idea
- Message hierarchy per channel
- Content pillars and editorial angle
- Visual/tone direction

**Tools**: marketing-psychology skill, copywriting skill (strategy sections)
**Output**: Creative brief

---

## Phase 4: Plan

**Goal**: Map execution across channels with timing, budget, and owners.

**Actions**:
- Campaign calendar with exact dates
- Channel strategy and budget allocation
- KPIs and success metrics for each channel
- Resource plan (who does what)

**Tools**: paid-advertising skill, content-strategy skill
**Output**: Campaign plan document

---

## Phase 5: Create

**Goal**: Produce all campaign assets.

**Actions by asset type**:
- **Web copy**: Use copywriting skill
- **Email sequences**: Use email-sequence skill
- **Social content**: Use social-content skill
- **Ad creative**: Use ad-creative skill
- **Sales collateral**: Use sales-enablement skill
- **SEO content**: Use seo-mastery + copywriting skills
- **Landing pages**: Use page-cro + copywriting skills

**Output**: All campaign assets ready for review

---

## Phase 6: Edit

**Goal**: Quality control and optimization before launch.

**Actions**:
- Copy editing pass on all written assets (use copy-editing skill)
- CRO review of landing pages (use page-cro skill)
- A/B variants for highest-impact elements (use ab-test-setup skill)
- Brand consistency check

**Output**: Finalized, approved assets

---

## Phase 7: Publish

**Goal**: Launch across all channels with coordinated timing.

**Launch sequence**:
1. Internal communication first (team briefing)
2. Owned channels (email list, in-app notification) — 2 hours before
3. Earned/social (organic posts) — launch moment
4. Paid amplification — launch moment or day 2 (after organic engagement builds)
5. PR/press (if applicable) — coordinated with embargo break

**Tracking setup** (must be done pre-launch):
- UTM parameters on all campaign links
- Conversion events firing in analytics
- Dashboard or report template ready

**Output**: Live campaign with full tracking active

---

## Phase 8: Measure

**Goal**: Evaluate performance and extract learnings for next campaign.

**Weekly review**:
- Traffic and conversions by channel
- CPA and ROAS by channel
- A/B test results (if applicable)
- Anomalies and issues

**Post-campaign analysis** (30 days after launch):
- Did we hit KPIs?
- Which channels beat target? Which missed?
- What would we do differently?
- What to test next?

**Tools**: analytics-attribution skill
**Output**: Campaign retrospective with learnings + next-steps recommendations

---

## Abbreviated Workflow for Smaller Projects

For single-asset projects (one landing page, one email sequence, etc.):

1. **Context** → Check product-marketing-context.md if it exists
2. **Clarify** → Confirm goal, audience, and CTA (5 questions max)
3. **Execute** → Use the appropriate skill from skills-catalog.md
4. **Review** → Quick quality check against skill's guidelines
5. **Deliver** → Present with brief rationale for key decisions

---

## Workflow for Ongoing Marketing Operations

### Daily
- Social media publishing and engagement
- Email replies and support escalations
- Ad budget monitoring (pause anything dramatically over CPA)

### Weekly
- Analytics review: traffic, conversions, CAC by channel
- New content published
- A/B test check-in (don't stop early)

### Monthly
- Full analytics report vs KPIs
- Content strategy refresh — new opportunities from SEO data
- Lead quality review with sales
- Budget reallocation based on performance

### Quarterly
- Strategy review and ICP refinement
- Brand/positioning audit
- Competitive landscape update
- Annual budget planning (if Q4)
